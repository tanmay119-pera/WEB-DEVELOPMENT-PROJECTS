My vision for The Living Archive

Most space websites feel like textbooks: dense, flat, and forgettable. I wanted to build something that feels like standing inside an observatory. Every world gets its own story, its own data, and its own motion.

It's built for everyone: a ten-year-old can play the quiz and a curious adult can dive into the telemetry.

“This is only the first chapter. I’ll keep growing the archive, one world at a time.”
